How we hire

Verified before joining

Identity and reference checks for every adult on campus, teaching or support staff alike.

Trained in early years

Teachers hold or are working towards an early-childhood qualification, with in-house training through the year.

First-aid aware

Staff know what to do in the first two minutes of an injury, a choking incident or a seizure.

Meet the teachers in person

Visit on a working morning and watch how the adults speak to the children. It tells you more than a qualification list.
