Programmes

Playgroup to Senior KG, plus daycare

Four preschool years for ages 2 to 6, and a daycare that runs alongside them until 7 pm. Children are placed by age as on 1st June of the academic year, with some flexibility for readiness.

Playgroup

2 to 3 years

Timing: 9:30 am to 12:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

The gentlest year. The goal is not academics — it is that your child walks in happily, separates from you without distress, and starts to trust an adult who is not family.

  • Sensory play: sand, water, dough, paint
  • Rhymes, action songs and simple stories
  • Toilet independence and self-feeding
  • Gross motor play — climbing, balancing, throwing

Nursery

3 to 4 years

Timing: 9:30 am to 12:30 pm, Monday to Friday.

Language takes off this year. Children move from single words to full sentences, and from parallel play to actual friendships.

  • Letter sounds and picture reading
  • Counting to 20, sorting, matching, patterns
  • Colours, shapes, seasons, community helpers
  • Sharing, turn-taking and simple classroom rules

Junior KG

4 to 5 years

Timing: 9:00 am to 12:30 pm, Monday to Friday.

Formal writing begins — carefully, and only after months of pre-writing strokes, threading, cutting and clay work have prepared the hand.

  • Capital and small letter formation
  • Blending sounds into three-letter words
  • Numbers to 50, more and less, simple addition
  • Show-and-tell, and first attempts at speaking to a group

Senior KG

5 to 6 years

Timing: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

The bridge to Grade 1. By the end of the year most children read short sentences, write independently and sit through a structured task without needing an adult beside them.

  • Reading simple sentences and short passages
  • Independent writing, dictation, sight words
  • Numbers to 100, addition and subtraction
  • School-readiness routines and Grade 1 interview practice

Daycare

Care that carries on until 7 pm

Daycare runs from the end of the school session until 7:00 pm, six days a week, and can be taken full-time or on selected days. It is a structured afternoon, not a waiting room.

  • Lunch supervision and a hygiene routine
  • A proper nap period with mats and a darkened room
  • Quiet activity, free play and outdoor time
  • Snack, and a settling-down period before pick-up

Non-school-going siblings up to 6 years can be enrolled for daycare alone, subject to seats.

A day with us

What a morning actually looks like

Times shift a little by age group, but the shape of the day stays the same — predictable enough to feel safe, varied enough to stay interesting.

  1. Arrival and free play

    Children settle in at their own pace, greet their teacher and choose an activity while everyone arrives.

  2. Circle time

    The calendar, weather, news from home, a rhyme and the theme of the day. This is where most of the talking happens.

  3. Concept session

    The focused block: language or numbers, taught with materials the children handle themselves.

  4. Snack and washroom routine

    Hands washed, tiffin opened by the child wherever possible, plates cleared afterwards.

  5. Activity and outdoor play

    Art, music, movement, story or gross-motor play outdoors before the day winds down.
