Admissions

Admissions open for 2026–27

Mid-term admissions are also accepted through the year, subject to seats in the relevant group. Seats are limited by our 1:10 ratio, so we fill each room and then stop.

For the school: confirm the dates and fee details below against your actual calendar before publishing. Nothing here should stay generic once you have the real numbers.

How admission works

  1. Send an enquiry

    Call, WhatsApp or fill the enquiry form. Tell us your child’s date of birth and which programme you are considering, and we will confirm seat availability and share the fee structure.

  2. Visit the campus

    Come on a working morning between 9:30 am and 12:30 pm so you can see the classrooms in use. Bring your child — how they react to the room is useful information.

  3. Meet the principal

    A short, informal conversation about your child’s routine, health, temperament and what you are hoping for. There is no child assessment or entrance test at this age.

  4. Submit the form and documents

    Complete the admission form and hand over the documents listed below, along with the registration fee.

  5. Confirm the seat and settle in

    Once fees are paid the seat is confirmed. New children begin with a short settling-in week of reduced hours, with a parent nearby if needed.

Documents required

  • Birth certificate of the child (photocopy)
  • Four recent passport-size photographs of the child
  • Aadhaar card of the child, if issued
  • Aadhaar or photo ID of both parents
  • Address proof
  • Immunisation record
  • Blood group and any medical or allergy details in writing
  • Photographs of everyone authorised to collect the child

Age criteria

Age is calculated as on 1st June of the academic year, with some flexibility for readiness.

  • Playgroup — 2 to 3 years
  • Nursery — 3 to 4 years
  • Junior KG — 4 to 5 years
  • Senior KG — 5 to 6 years
  • Daycare only — up to 6 years

If your child sits on the boundary between two groups, talk to us. Placement should follow readiness, not just the calendar.

Academic year

June to April, following the Pune school calendar. Summer break in May, with a separate summer camp offered.

Questions parents ask before enrolling

Do you take mid-term admissions?

Yes, throughout the year, as long as a seat is free in that age group. Children who join mid-term get the same settling-in week as everyone else.

Is there an entrance test?

No. There is no test or assessment for admission. We meet you and your child, and that is the whole process.

My child is not toilet trained yet. Is that a problem?

Not for Playgroup. Many two-year-olds are still learning, and we work on it with you. Please send spare clothes.

Can we take daycare without the preschool programme?

Yes, daycare alone is available for children up to six years, subject to seats.
