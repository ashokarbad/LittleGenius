For the school — important: the quotes below are blank on purpose. Publishing invented reviews would breach the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) rules and ASCI’s code on misleading advertising, and parents recognise fake testimonials instantly. Collect three or four short quotes over WhatsApp, ask permission to publish with a first name and class, and paste them in. Better still, ask those same parents to leave the review on your Google Business Profile, where it also helps your local search ranking.

Rather hear it first-hand?

Ask us to put you in touch with a parent from the class your child would join. Most are happy to take a five-minute call, and they will tell you things a website never will.
