Facilities

Everything at child height

A preschool building should let a four-year-old do as much as possible without asking an adult for help. That principle decided most of what is here.

Bright, smart classrooms

Naturally lit rooms with washable surfaces, rounded corners, low open shelving and child-sized furniture. Every room has a display board at the children’s eye level, not the teacher’s.

An audio-visual setup supports rhymes, stories and concept videos — used in short, planned doses rather than as a way to keep a room quiet.

Indoor and outdoor play

A well-equipped indoor play zone with soft flooring keeps the day moving through the Pune monsoon, with a ball pool, slides and a soft-block area.

Outdoors there is space for climbing, riding, running and sand play, supervised at all times, with shade for the summer months.

In-house library

Picture books, board books and early readers on shelves children reach themselves. Each child borrows a book weekly to read at home with a parent — the single highest-return ten minutes in a preschooler’s day.

Activity and art zone

A dedicated messy area for paint, clay, water and sand, so art does not have to be scaled down to protect the furniture.

Safety and security

What we do about safety, specifically

Vague reassurance is easy. Here is what actually happens.

CCTV coverage

Cameras across classrooms, corridors and play areas, with footage retained and reviewable by the management on request.

Single supervised entry

One gate, staffed through school hours, and no child released to anyone who is not on the authorised pick-up list.

Verified staff

Identity and reference checks before joining. All adults on campus, including support staff, are known and accounted for.

Paediatric first aid

A stocked first-aid kit, staff trained to use it, and an immediate call to parents for anything beyond a scraped knee — every time.

Daily hygiene

Sanitised toys and surfaces, supervised hand-washing before and after eating, and separate child-height washrooms.

Illness policy

Children with fever, conjunctivitis or a contagious infection stay home. It feels strict on the day and saves the whole class a fortnight later.
