Welcome

The first step to a better future starts here

We aim to shape the minds of our little geniuses by equipping them with critical thinking skills, a global perspective, and respect for the core values of honesty, loyalty and compassion.

Developing these skills is the cornerstone of the education we offer. In practice that means a child at Li’l Genius spends the morning building, sorting, questioning, singing and getting messy — and goes home able to do a little more on their own than they could yesterday.

We are a single-centre neighbourhood school, not a franchise. The person who answers your call is the person who will know your child’s name.

Why parents choose us

Six things we get right, every day

Small promises, kept consistently, matter more to a three-year-old than a long list of features.

Personal attention

A 1:10 teacher-to-child ratio means every child is spoken to by name, several times a day. Shy children are not left at the edge of the room.

A safe campus

CCTV-monitored classrooms and play areas, a single supervised entry point, rounded furniture, and staff verification before anyone joins us.

Learning through activity

Concepts arrive through sand, water, blocks, music and stories first. Pencil work comes later, and only when small hands are ready for it.

Daycare that fits work

Open until 7 pm, six days a week, with rest, a hot meal routine and quiet time built in — not just a room to wait in.

Trained, steady faculty

Early-years trained teachers who stay. Low staff turnover is the single most underrated thing you can look for in a preschool.

Parents in the loop

Daily updates, an open-door policy for visits, and honest termly conversations about what your child is finding easy and what they are not.

Programmes

Four steps, ages two to six

Each year has its own room, its own rhythm and its own goals. Children move up when they are ready for the next one.

Playgroup

2–3 yrs

Settling in, separation from parents, first friendships, toilet independence and lots of sensory play.

Nursery

3–4 yrs

Spoken language, sounds, counting to twenty, colours and shapes, and learning to take turns.

Junior KG

4–5 yrs

Letter formation, blending sounds into words, number sense to fifty, and simple science questions.

Senior KG

5–6 yrs

Reading short sentences, writing independently, addition and subtraction, and readiness for Grade 1.

Facilities

Built at child height

Child-friendly infrastructure

Low shelves children can reach without asking, washable surfaces, natural light, and furniture sized for two-year-olds.

Well-equipped play zone

An indoor soft-play area for monsoon days and an outdoor space for climbing, riding and running.

In-house library

Picture books children may choose themselves, plus a weekly take-home book to read with a parent.

Audio-visual centre

Used sparingly and with purpose — rhymes, stories and concept videos, never as a way to fill time.

CCTV monitoring

Classrooms, corridors and play areas are covered, with footage retained and available to the management.

Hygiene and first aid

Daily sanitisation, supervised washroom routines, and staff trained in paediatric first aid.
